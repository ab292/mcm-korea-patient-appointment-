Complete Patient Guide

Step-by-step guide for your hospital visit

1

Before Your Visit

What to Bring:

  • Valid ID: Passport, Driver's License, National ID, or Kebele ID
  • Insurance Documents: Card and policy papers
  • Medical Records: Previous reports and test results
  • Medication List: Current medicines with dosages
  • Referral Letter: If referred by another doctor
  • Emergency Contact: Phone numbers of family members
  • Payment Method: Cash, card, or mobile money

Preparation Tips:

Confirm Appointment

Call 24 hours before to confirm

Fasting Instructions

Follow if required for tests

Arrive Early

30 minutes before scheduled time

Face Mask

Required for infection control

2

During Your Visit

①
Check-in at Reception

Present your ID and appointment confirmation. Receive patient wristband.

②
Vital Signs Check

Nurses check blood pressure, temperature, and pulse in triage area.

③
Doctor Consultation

Meet your doctor for examination and treatment planning.

④
Tests & Pharmacy

Proceed to lab/imaging if needed. Collect medications from pharmacy.

⑤
Billing & Discharge

Complete payment. Receive discharge instructions and follow-up details.

3

After Your Visit

Follow-up Instructions

  • Medications: Take as prescribed exactly
  • Appointments: Schedule follow-ups if needed
  • Test Results: Collect within 24-48 hours
  • Emergency Contact: Call if symptoms worsen
  • Diet: Follow dietary recommendations
  • Rest: Get adequate rest as advised

Insurance & Billing Information

Accepted Insurance Providers

Insurance Claim Process:

  1. Present valid insurance card at registration
  2. Provide necessary authorization forms
  3. Pay any applicable co-payments or deductibles
  4. We handle direct billing with your insurance company
  5. Receive statement for any remaining balance
