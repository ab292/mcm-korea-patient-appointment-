About Korea General Hospital

Excellence in Healthcare Since 2010

Our Story

Established in 2010 through a collaborative initiative between Korean medical experts and Ethiopian healthcare professionals, Korea General Hospital has grown to become a premier healthcare institution in Addis Ababa.

Located in the heart of Gerji, our hospital was founded with a singular vision: to bring world-class Korean medical standards to Ethiopia while respecting and understanding the unique healthcare needs of our community.

Over the past decade, we have served over 500,000 patients, performed more than 50,000 successful surgeries, and built a reputation for excellence in patient care and medical innovation.

Hospital Building

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality, and compassionate healthcare services that improve the lives of our patients and strengthen our community through Korean-Ethiopian medical collaboration.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's leading healthcare institution, recognized for clinical excellence, innovative treatments, and outstanding patient outcomes by 2030.
